My Notes

Artificial Intelligence: AI is the field of creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
Machine Learning: Machine Learning is a branch of AI where systems learn patterns from data.
Deep Learning: Deep Learning uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns.

Key Takeaway

AI is a broad field. Machine Learning is a subset of AI, and Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning.
